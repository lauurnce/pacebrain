# Study notes — L1 and L2 penalties

Both shrink weights. They differ in *where* they shrink them to, and the
geometry explains the whole difference.

**L2** penalises squared magnitude. The gradient of the penalty is proportional
to the weight, so it shrinks large weights hard and small ones barely at all.
Nothing reaches exactly zero.

**L1** penalises absolute magnitude. The gradient is constant regardless of
size, so small weights get pushed to exactly zero and stay there. That is
feature selection as a side effect of the penalty.

## The picture

The L1 constraint region is a diamond with corners on the axes; the L2 region is
a circle. An optimum is far more likely to land on a corner than on a smooth
arc, and a corner means a coordinate is zero.

## In practice

Elastic net uses both, which handles the case L1 does badly: among correlated
features L1 picks one arbitrarily and zeroes the rest, while the L2 component
keeps them together.
