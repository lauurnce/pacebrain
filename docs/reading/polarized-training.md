# Seiler on intensity distribution

Observations that elite endurance athletes spend the large majority of training
at low intensity and a small fraction at high, with relatively little in the
middle.

The proposed mechanism is that moderate intensity accumulates autonomic fatigue
disproportionate to its adaptive stimulus, so it costs recovery capacity that
the hard sessions need.

Worth holding loosely — the descriptive finding about what elites do is stronger
evidence than the prescriptive claim that others should copy it.
