# ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs — Krizhevsky et al., 2012

The result usually credited with starting the current era.

What is striking reading it now is how much of the contribution is engineering:
GPU implementation, ReLU instead of saturating activations, dropout, and
aggressive data augmentation. The architecture is not conceptually novel.

The lesson I take is that the ideas were largely available and what changed was
compute plus a handful of practical tricks becoming standard at once.
