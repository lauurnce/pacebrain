# Understanding Deep Learning Requires Rethinking Generalization — Zhang et al., 2016

Shows large networks can fit entirely random labels to zero training error.

That is decisive against explanations of generalisation based on capacity alone:
the same architecture that memorises noise also generalises well on real data,
so capacity cannot be what distinguishes the two cases.

Left the field without a working theory of why deep networks generalise, which
is roughly still the position. A useful paper for being clear about what it
disproved rather than what it established.
