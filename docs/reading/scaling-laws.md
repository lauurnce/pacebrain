# Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models — Kaplan et al., 2020

Finds loss falls as a smooth power law in parameters, data and compute across
many orders of magnitude.

The practical value is predictability: performance at a larger scale can be
extrapolated before paying for it, which turns architecture and budget decisions
into arithmetic.

Power-law means diminishing returns in the harshest form — each equal
fractional improvement costs a constant multiple more resource.
