# Learning rate

The step size applied to each parameter update. The single most important hyperparameter: too high and training diverges, too low and it stalls or crawls.

In PaceBrain, Learning rate matters at exactly the boundary between baselines and fancier models — it is one of the knobs that decides whether added complexity earns its keep.
