# Periodization

Structuring training into blocks with different emphases — base, build, peak, taper — so that adaptation and recovery are planned rather than incidental. The blocks exist because the adaptations conflict: high volume and high intensity cannot both be maximised at once without accumulating more fatigue than the athlete absorbs.
