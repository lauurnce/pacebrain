# Heart rate zones

Effort bands defined as percentages of maximum or threshold heart rate, used to keep easy days easy and hard days hard. Their weakness is drift: heart rate lags effort at the start of a run and climbs at a fixed pace as fatigue and heat accumulate, so a zone can be met while the actual effort is wrong.
