# Hill repeats

Repeated hard efforts up a gradient with easy recovery back down. The incline limits the speed reachable, so they deliver a strong strength and aerobic stimulus at lower impact than equivalent flat intervals — which is why they often appear early in a block, before the legs are ready for track work.
