# RPE

Rating of perceived exertion, a subjective effort scale. Its advantage over pace and heart rate is that it needs no device and automatically accounts for heat, terrain and fatigue — the exact conditions under which the objective measures mislead.

In PaceBrain, Rpe matters at exactly the boundary between baselines and fancier models — it is one of the knobs that decides whether added complexity earns its keep.
