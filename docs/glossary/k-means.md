# K-means

Partitioning data into k clusters by alternately assigning points to the nearest centre and recomputing centres. It assumes roughly spherical clusters of similar size, and k has to be chosen in advance by something other than the algorithm.

The failure mode to watch with K means is silent optimism: results improve on the validation set for reasons that will not survive contact with next month's data.
