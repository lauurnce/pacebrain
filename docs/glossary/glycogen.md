# Glycogen

Carbohydrate stored in muscle and liver, the body's fast-access fuel. Stores hold roughly 90 to 120 minutes of hard running, which is why marathon pacing and mid-race fuelling matter and shorter races do not need either.

Practical trap with Glycogen: comparing raw values across athletes without adjusting for training age, volume and conditions turns a measurement into a leaderboard.
