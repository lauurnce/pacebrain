# Central limit theorem

The result that sums of many independent variables tend toward a normal distribution regardless of their individual shapes. It is why normality is a reasonable default for aggregates and a poor one for raw measurements.

The failure mode to watch with Central limit theorem is silent optimism: results improve on the validation set for reasons that will not survive contact with next month's data.
