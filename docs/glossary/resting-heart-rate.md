# Resting heart rate

Heart rate measured at complete rest, falling as aerobic fitness improves. Its day-to-day value is as a warning signal: an elevated morning reading often precedes illness or signals incomplete recovery.

Practical trap with Resting heart rate: comparing raw values across athletes without adjusting for training age, volume and conditions turns a measurement into a leaderboard.
