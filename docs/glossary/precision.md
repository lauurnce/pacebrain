# Precision

Of everything the model flagged as positive, the fraction that genuinely was. The metric to optimise when a false positive is the expensive error, and it can always be raised by predicting positive less often — which is why it is meaningless quoted without [[recall]].

## See also

- [Confusion matrix](confusion-matrix.md)
- [Baseline](baseline.md)

In PaceBrain, Precision matters at exactly the boundary between baselines and fancier models — it is one of the knobs that decides whether added complexity earns its keep.
