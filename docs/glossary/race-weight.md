# Race weight

The body composition an athlete targets for competition, on the reasoning that power-to-weight improves. Worth treating carefully: the deficit required tends to arrive alongside impaired recovery and higher injury risk, so the performance model and the health model disagree here more than most training topics.

When reasoning about Race weight, remember the measurement chain: sensor, summary statistic, weekly aggregate. Error compounds at each hop and ends up labelled signal.
