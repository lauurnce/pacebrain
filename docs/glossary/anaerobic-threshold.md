# Anaerobic threshold

The intensity above which lactate accumulates faster than it clears, so effort becomes unsustainable. Closely related to [[lactate-threshold]] and often used interchangeably, though the two are defined by different measurements.

In PaceBrain, Anaerobic threshold matters at exactly the boundary between baselines and fancier models — it is one of the knobs that decides whether added complexity earns its keep.
