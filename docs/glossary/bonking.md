# Bonking

The abrupt collapse in pace when [[glycogen]] runs out and the body falls back on fat metabolism, which cannot support race intensity. Distinct from ordinary fatigue in how sudden it is, and it is a fuelling and pacing failure rather than a fitness one.

In PaceBrain terms, Bonking shows up whenever weekly logs are turned into features — the textbook definition is crisp, real training data is not, and the gap is where the modelling lives.
